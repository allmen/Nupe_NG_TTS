36.Kamina ubea egi ma na, uci gan: Tsoci, akika miu'ma yinzagi, Soko ma kpe yan na uma na gan wyun tso, Egi bagi ma ufe dozhin yinzagia. Akika, mi ma'u tu sunna Maryamu (egi na'a wun Soko gbaugi na), mi mae wa tswitswa ya'u be wo yin, to zuriya'uzhi, wo tswa'a be egun shetan yin, nayi eza cice yaci na.
