15.Gan gan, kashi min yaye labari yan natsozhi de alheri gan wuncinzhina? Ya zanazhi ga sundan Soko na, Aljanna nazhi nuwan zhieje fi tako'abo na danbo ya'a eba yan Tsociabo, bagabo ma'afi tutunyin'o, To yinmizhi nazhi yi lilicizhina, ganni be yeda jin dagan eba Soko. Soko mae le wuzhizhi ye (be iri yan nazhi e'jin na.)
